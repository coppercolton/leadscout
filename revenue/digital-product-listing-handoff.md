## Product title

Missed-Call Recovery Audit Kit for Local Service Businesses

## Short description

A practical, source-linked worksheet and report template for reviewing the public phone, contact-form, quote/valuation, chat, and after-hours paths that turn inbound interest into a human response.

## Buyer outcome

The buyer receives a repeatable way to document observable response-path friction, attach evidence, label confidence, and sequence a small set of fixes. This is an operating template, not a promise of increased leads, revenue, ranking, or response-time improvement.

## What is included

1. `revenue/audit-fulfillment-template.md` — reusable audit/report structure.
2. `revenue/audit-intake-tracker.csv` — starter tracker for intake, evidence, delivery, and funnel state.
3. `public/sample-audit.html` — clearly illustrative sample showing the evidence log, confidence labels, and priority sequence.
4. `public/missed-call-recovery.html` — request-ready example page with a no-auto-send form.

## Suggested listing copy

### Full description

Inbound prospects often encounter several public paths before they reach a person: a phone number, a contact form, a quote or valuation route, a chat widget, and after-hours instructions. This kit gives you one consistent review process for those paths.

Use it to:

- record the exact public route reviewed;
- attach a URL, screenshot, timestamp, or clearly labeled inference;
- separate observed facts from assumptions;
- assign confidence to each finding;
- organize a three-priority fix sequence; and
- track intake, delivery, reply state, and payment evidence separately.

The included sample is illustrative only. It is not a real customer result and contains no measured performance claim.

### Best fit

Local service operators, real-estate teams, agencies, and consultants who want a lightweight, evidence-first intake and audit workflow without buying a CRM or lead-generation subscription.
