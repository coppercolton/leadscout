# LeadScout Missed-Call Recovery Audit

## Offer
A 24-hour public-path audit for a real-estate team or local business. We inspect phone, contact, valuation, chat, and after-hours response paths and return evidence plus a prioritized fix plan.

## Why this first
- No paid acquisition required.
- Existing LeadScout and Austin outreach assets can deliver it.
- Can be fulfilled with browser research and a repeatable template.
- Can convert into implementation work or a downloadable audit template later.

## Fulfillment checklist
1. Confirm public website and business identity.
2. Test phone, form, valuation, chat, and after-hours paths without impersonating a customer or submitting deceptive data.
3. Record visible response expectations, dead ends, delays, and handoff gaps.
4. Capture evidence links/screenshots and timestamp each observation.
5. Produce the one-page leak map and three-priority fix sequence.
6. Send the audit and offer optional implementation only if requested.

## Measurement
- Requests received
- Audits delivered
- Positive replies
- Paid audits or implementation payments verified
- Time-to-fulfill
- Repeatable findings by niche

## Safety
Do not submit fake leads, make purchases, impersonate prospects, or claim a business has a defect without evidence. Outreach must be small, personalized, and include an opt-out path where applicable.
